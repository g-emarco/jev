# Takeaways

1. Decisions need **typed outputs and probabilities**, not prose
2. Put them in **middleware**: classify early, gate tools, route models
3. Use the **cheapest decider that clears the bar**, with a human below the threshold
4. Google offers models, an agent platform, and **decision engines**: pair them
5. Pin alpha APIs, track retirements, **measure on your own data**
